My name is Wylie Chang 張振曜. I am a researcher in the Department of Emergency Medicine at Massachusetts General Hospital in the Emergency Medical Services group. I study emergency medicine, with a particular focus on shared decision-making in psychiatric emergencies. My research is guided by questions about social order, interpersonal cooperation, and the exercise of agency within medical institutions. I maintain interests in the organization, governance, and legitimacy of institutions and the history of science and medicine. Before turning toward medicine, I led several civic organizations, including Democracy House, Register2Vote.org, and Lead for America. I completed science coursework at Harvard University and read Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at Tufts University.
